# node-x11 — June 2011

A pure-JavaScript X11 protocol client. No bindings, no `node-gyp`, no Xlib —
just the protocol, encoded and decoded in JS.

^^^

```js
const x11 = require('x11');
const display = await x11.createClient();
const wid = display.client.AllocID();
display.client.CreateWindow(wid, root, 0, 0, 400, 300);
display.client.MapWindow(wid);
```

^^^

That was fifteen years ago. And for fifteen years it stayed exactly that: a
*protocol library*. You could open a window. You could fill a rectangle. And
then you were on your own.

^^^

Because **opening a window is not a toolkit.**

Before we work out what is — it is worth ten minutes on what that library
actually talks to. Three ideas, and then we watch them on the wire.
