  DEMO — 2 min. Click into the editor and edit something small and obvious:
  a heading, a colour in the <style> block. It re-renders as you type.
  Say what it is NOT: no browser, no webview, no Chromium. Parser, cascade,
  layout, paint — in this process, on this window, by the same toolkit
  drawing the slide around it.
  Esc hands the keyboard back to the deck. Say that out loud before you
  click in, or the arrow keys will not move the deck and you will look lost.
  The editor's HTML mode was written for this slide — that is the "find the
  gap" loop, three slides early.
