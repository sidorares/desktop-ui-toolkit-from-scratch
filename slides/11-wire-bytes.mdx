# Three shapes, and every byte accounted for

<Wire example="title" />

^^^

A **value list** — a mask, then one 32-bit value per bit, in mask order. A
**string** — 8-bit units, padded up to a multiple of four, with a length
that counts the characters and not the padding. And the one request that
asks a question, whose reply carries its **sequence number**.
